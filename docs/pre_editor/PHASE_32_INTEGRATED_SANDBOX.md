## Chapter 24 coverage

Gregory Hodges brings the rigid bodies and constraints together on printed pages 93–100 of *Game Physics: The Rest of Your Life*, v1.08. The GEngine scene uses the existing interactive Sandbox's coordinate convention, room, materials and camera. The table describes what can be observed in this scene. Provider qualification remains attached to the individual workloads in the chapter ledger. [Chapter 24](https://gamephysicsweekend.github.io/pdfs/GamePhysicsTheRestOfYourLife.pdf#page=93) · [Chapter-by-chapter evidence](PHASE_32_BOOK_CONFORMANCE.md#chapter-ledger)

| Demonstration | Existing construction used | Integrated coverage and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Rigid-body stack | Box Stack | Sixteen original two-metre wood boxes, in four columns, settle on the common floor. |
| Sphere and convex hull | Sphere Diamond | The original sharp diamond and sphere use their original opposing velocities and angular motion. They occupy a separate lane. | Chain | Distance Chain, four links | One static root and four dynamic spheres. The links retain their 2.25 m target distances, connected-pair rule, materials and scheduled tip impulse. |
| Ball-and-socket | Anchored Swing | Two spheres joined at their original local pivots, with the standalone swing impulse. Its standalone gravity is −12 m/s². The combined room uses −10 m/s² for all bodies. |
| Hinge and limited hinge | Basic Hinge and Limited Hinge | Each two-body assembly retains its local frames and its own provider joint. The limited hinge retains its ±45° interval. Quaternion Hinge remains individually selectable. |
| Constant velocity | Constant Velocity and Limited Constant Velocity | Both Chapter 19 and Chapter 20 providers have distinct endpoints and handles. The original angular packets supply the limits and the scheduled impulses. |
| Passive ragdoll | Passive Ragdoll | Six bodies and five joints, at the original reference origin. **Experimental for visual and development use on Jolt and Native.** |
| Rotary motor | Motors, Free | The original 100 kg bar, authored box material and powered hinge. The drive retains its 2 rad/s target and 100 N·m torque cap. |
| Moving platform and rider | Movers | Original kinematic platform, dynamic Wood rider and simulation-time sine path. A 13 m shift in X moves the path center and rider together. The 16 m amplitude and platform half-width give a travel envelope of Z −19 to +19 m at X 20–26 m. |
| Orientation-only constraint | No delivered standalone provider | The book also holds the relative orientation of two boxes. GEngine's public joint families cover distance, ball-and-socket, hinge and the two CV variants. Full relative-orientation locking remains unsupported here. |
| Boundaries | Existing interactive Sandbox | One floor, four walls, one shared light rig, skybox and Editor camera. Hierarchy groups are display metadata. Group membership creates no Transform parent or Physics collision filtering. |

The room uses one Scene and its 60 Hz scheduler. A shared downward gravity of 10 m/s² matches the reference mover and ragdoll. The standalone room, its free contacts and the distance and swing examples use downward gravity of 12 m/s². The gravity setting applies to every body in the integrated room. The object builders first create entities and author their original fixtures and renderables. After the Scene starts its Physics world, the builders resolve the actual entity-to-body bindings and install 15 joints plus the platform controller. The five angular assemblies have separate state. All ordinary objects share the same rendering resource owner. Each ragdoll bone retains its authored rust UV layout and independent material instance. Original texture bindings, tiling and PBR parameters come from the standalone constructors. [Scene construction](../../RigidBodySimulation/src/RbsScene.cpp)
