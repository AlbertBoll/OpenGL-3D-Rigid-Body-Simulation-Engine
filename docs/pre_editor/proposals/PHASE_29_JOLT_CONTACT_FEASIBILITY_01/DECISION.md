# Phase 29 · Jolt contact-integration feasibility decision 01

**Decision: the configured Jolt 5.6.0 public integration does not satisfy all three contact requirements.** The smallest SDK extension worth pursuing is an opt-in continuous-contact path for the six registered bones and their five room bodies. It needs to own feature detection, impact response and the remainder of the step together. An additional callback alone cannot do this. The proposed path has a finite rejection policy. Its ability to complete DROP and TILT within that policy and the frozen cost limits is unproved. **Keep the final DROP replacement and all seven proposed roles unreleased.**

The source reviewed is upstream commit `e77f175595e64cb44218cc9d9d56fc365ad0e36a` with the configured `gengine-contact-resistance-01` extension. The repository's read-only pin verifier reproduced that identity. Option B and the additive visual method remain active. ## One proposed SDK extension

1. **Register the actual collision domain.** After the ordinary scene or fixture has completed creation, parameter restoration and provider readback, pass six bone IDs, five joint IDs and five room IDs with their world and generations. The scene already checks for eleven bodies. Register atomically at an idle owner boundary. Recheck membership, filters and shape revision on edits. Disconnect before retirement, world close or failed replacement. Keep the prior valid registration on a failed authoring transaction. Thirty bone/room pairs and fifteen bone/bone pairs bound the domain. The reference enables forty of those forty-five. Support the editable connected-pair flags, Dynamic/Kinematic/Static bones, authored box centers, boxes, spheres and capsules, finite room edges and corners. A kinematic bone can split a stock island. Schedule the **entire registered domain** behind one barrier even then. Sleep and wake remain actual provider decisions. If an admitted interaction can add an external collidable actor or move a room body outside this domain, establish a bounded handover before accepting the design. Silently excluding that actor or disabling the interaction would break compatibility. [Scene construction](../../../../RigidBodySimulation/src/RbsScene.cpp#L1681) · [Authored geometry and motion types](../../../../GEngine/src/Physics/PassiveRagdoll.cpp#L53)
2. **Predict before advancing.** After applying external forces/gyro once and projecting the current joint/contact velocities, sweep each pair from its common starting time over the remaining interval. Use outward-rounded swept AABBs, then finite primitive features. For a box with COM-relative radius bound R, expand the translated starting bounds by at least 2R sin(min(π, |ω|h)/2). Use exact sphere and capsule core primitives with their shell radius and actual COM geometry. Enumerate vertex/vertex, vertex/edge, vertex/face and edge/edge witnesses as applicable, including reversed ownership, finite face/segment validity, parallel/coincident limits and endcaps. A box/box stream has at most 64 + 192 + 96 + 144 = 496 such feature keys. Handle face overlap and simultaneous/degenerate contact through clipping and deterministic manifold reduction. Resting contact cannot mask a different approaching feature. For existing features certify continuation/separation and bounded deepening. Retain a distinct time bracket for each possible new impact.
3. **Certify the next time of contact.** For held twists the relative speed of any two material points is at most |vA−vB| + RA|ωA| + RB|ωB|. A positive lower distance bound larger than this rate times the remaining interval excludes a crossing. Use interval bounds on *finite* features, their validity domains and signed support for existing overlaps. Include floating-point error in bounds. Subdivide uncertain intervals with fixed depth-first storage. Advance only through certified-clear time or to a physically supported incoming contact with a bracket whose spatial uncertainty is justified from numerical precision. An overlap of time brackets alone cannot establish simultaneity. Refine ordering or certify all tied patches at the common pose. Unresolved tangency, nonfinite arithmetic, degeneracy or exhausted budget yields a typed failure. Do not jump over an uncertain interval by enforcing a minimum forward step. The common-time trajectory is a proposed registered-domain integration change; the historical SDK wrote rotation before translation.
4. **One response owner.** At the accepted event advance the registered bodies to a common time, assemble simultaneous patches and evaluate the actual Jolt material settings at their true pose and incoming velocity. Use the existing joint and contact row math, including Design 02's two coupled Book-CV shoulders if admitted, with the original friction, restitution and spin/rolling response. Hold the current patch while resolving its replacement. Transfer only matched feature multipliers. Warm-start once per outer tick. Carry total impulse and apply its delta. Track restitution consumption per contact episode and treat coplanar manifold points as a patch, so a changed feature ID alone cannot earn a second rebound. Rearm only after certified separation and renewed closing. Preserve independent impacts at another feature. Re-linearize at the event and account for gravity/restitution bias once. Keep all impulse edits in a zero-time batch; invalidate previous trajectory certificates for **all six** after that batch and repeat the sweep before advancing. At the end sweep/validate position corrections and final poses before publishing. Duplicating a stock contact, losing unspent time, rejecting a genuine new bounce or stopping a rotating limb at its first touch would invalidate this design.
5. **Integrate with existing jobs and caches.** Use a caller-owned context via an opt-in Update overload. Leave the existing Update entry for unregistered worlds. Store the pointer on the outer update context, outside the Arena-backed Step. Give one worker exclusive mutation rights while all competing jobs for registered bodies wait. Filter registered pairs before normal cached-row creation, reserve the registered bodies' ordinary joint/velocity, integration, linear CCD and position work for that owner, and export final contact cache state **before** removed-callback processing. Complete sleep, broadphase bounds and wake bookkeeping exactly once. The ordinary job graph schedules cache removal separately from position solving. Establish an explicit ordering dependency on the existing jobs for the opted-in path. Never append contacts to an already finalized ordinary island table. Material callbacks run at accepted contacts, not after a deferred replay. Use internal fixed records for tentative events, and expose diagnostic samples as successful only on whole-step commit. A full observer buffer or unverifiable material callback blocks qualification. [Solver and warm start](../../../../external/jolt/Jolt/Physics/PhysicsSystem.cpp#L1530) · [Job ordering](../../../../external/jolt/Jolt/Physics/PhysicsSystem.cpp#L505) · [Cache finalization](../../../../external/jolt/Jolt/Physics/PhysicsSystem.cpp#L2508) · [Existing observer semantics](../../../../GEngine/src/Physics/JoltParameterEditor.inl#L32)

For the two recorded floor witnesses there is a simpler certificate. For a box vertex against a static planar face under a held twist, its height has the form `a + b t + c cos(w t) + d sin(w t)`. Split at its analytic derivative zeros, bracket the first signed crossing on each monotone interval and check the projected witness against the finite face. Coplanar faces at the first leg impact must produce one simultaneous patch. For two rotating primitives, unsigned set-distance bounds can reject disjoint finite features. Bounding a *signed* face-plane expression additionally requires bounding the rotating normal term in its derivative and the clipped witness domain. Ambiguous signs or a non-unique normal need a certified degeneracy branch. This is the main outstanding geometry proof beyond the archived floor examples.

The exact extension boundary proposed in the job graph is the existing single-owner CCD resolution job, after gravity and velocity setup and before position/sleep completion. The opted-in path must reserve the selected domain before ordinary velocity and integration jobs mutate its bodies. The domain owner runs its interleaved joint/contact work and all of its position validation there, then lets the ordinary jobs finish their bookkeeping. Move the selected cache-finalization dependency behind that owner's result without introducing a dependency cycle or reserving another job. Include inactive starts and the all-asleep/no-job fast path. Keep preset world time, one gravity/gyro application and no repeated damping or force compensation. At most two velocity sweeps for initial projection and two for each of four event groups is one possible deterministic allocation of the ten sweeps. It may make joint response too soft. Reuse of unspent sweeps must consume the same counters and require another certificate before advancing. A trajectory guard or budget exhaustion cannot turn a partially advanced frame into success. ### Work and storage ceiling for the design

These proposed caps bound work **including failures**. They grant no tolerance and need evidence that ordinary gameplay completes comfortably within them.

| One outer 1/60 s Update | Proposed hard design limit |
| --- | --- |
| Time and solver work | Four distinct impact-time batches, at most ten total velocity sweeps and two position sweeps across the entire domain, with at most one pass over each admitted row per sweep. No per-event reset of counters. Certify after any batch of impulses before advancing. At most eight full-domain scans in total, including post-position checks. |
| Geometry | At most 360 pair screens and 178,560 streamed feature-key visits (8 × 45 × 496). Separate shared limit of 32,768 interval-node evaluations per Update, depth 20. Each node uses fixed-size primitive predicates and bounded clipping of finite faces. Use no recursive unbounded GJK/EPA fallback. Exhaustion or an unresolved fifth independent support point after the SDK's four-point reduction fails the attempt if coverage cannot be certified. |
| Registered storage | One construction-owned slab capped at **96 KiB**. Budget 45 aligned contact rows at 752 bytes, two banks of 180 feature records at 48 bytes, 900 episode entries at 16 bytes, 11 body slots at 256 bytes, 45 pair slots at 64 bytes, 4 KiB for the shoulder workspaces and 4 KiB for streaming geometry. These sum to 79,408 bytes. The remaining 18,896 cover alignment, listener/fault bookkeeping and implementation detail. Require compiled layouts, high-water marks and a matching teardown free. Store no list of all feature keys. Zero additional per-Update heap calls/bytes, Arena sites or worker-job allocations for this path. Attribute existing SDK calls separately. |

The archived peak of **12,460,448** bytes was only **65,632** below the frozen **12,526,080** source envelope. The recorded maximum was 18 positive reservations against 19. The source already assigns all 19, including the conditional sorted-CCD pointer array. Even adding 16 bytes to each of 16,384 ordinary contact slots costs 262,144 bytes. Keep the 32 MiB Arena, all site identities and size limits, matched releases and zero end occupancy. Account for the slab as an explicit new construction cost. Prove steady heap attribution, including contacts, workers and teardown of the historical 229,376-byte JobSystem allocation. The archived DROP inclusive mean/p95 were 0.094/0.201 ms. They cannot price the new contact search. Charge the whole new path, both publication passes and observer work to the existing inclusive limits. The 8.333 ms mean/median and 12.500 ms p95/maximum sliding-60-mean limits plus clock rules apply separately to each workload. Preserve the same-tick observer limits. ## A failed step is terminal for its world

**Choose fail-stop and reconstruct from committed authored inputs.** A full SDK save/restore on every tick would gather body and manifold arrays, and public pose restoration omits internal cache, warm-start and sleep state. The Owner sees a typed failure, the attempt stays charged, and further simulation requires a fresh world/generation. **Recommendation.** Select this single SDK extension for architectural review only. Before an implementation or runtime request, demonstrate a certified finite-feature implementation for parallel/grazing contacts, persistence and simultaneous events on the supported primitives, exact single-application restitution and warm-start transfer, a feasible pass schedule for the coupled graph, compiled-size/build feasibility estimates and coverage of registration changes. The offline evidence establishes the three required insertion points and two geometric witnesses. It cannot establish convergence, CPU fit, positive completed-tick contact coverage or natural motion. A fail-stop limit reached in normal falling or settling is a rejected candidate.

The eventual consolidated request must price a verified SDK rebuild and the full source impact. Preserve the zero-tick Debug lifetime case and add explicit early/late fault injection, a failure on the second tick of a frame, stale access, clean shutdown and fresh-domain recovery.
