## Stock construction and contact behavior

Keep the six boxes, rest frames, mass/inertia, physical materials, gravity and timestep. Set the neck and both hip `HingeConstraintSettings` axes to frame X/Y with ±45° limits. Set both shoulder `SwingTwistConstraintSettings` twist/plane axes to X/Z, cone half-angles to 45°, twist limits to zero, motors off and joint friction torque zero. A full `RagdollSettings` would order a torso-root skeleton parent-first, assign six parts and five `mToParent` settings, then call `CreateRagdoll` and `AddToPhysicsSystem`. The wrapper creates bodies, stores their IDs and destroys them after removal. GEngine already binds six Scene-owned bodies and five PhysicsWorld-owned joints, with the neck ordered head→torso. Call the same stock settings' `Create(bodyA, bodyB)` on these bodies. This preserves signed endpoint order and member retirement. The wrapper adds instance vectors and pose helpers and uses the ordinary contact solver. Its `AddImpulse` targets every part. [Wrapper lifecycle](../../../external/jolt/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L406) · [Group impulse](../../../external/jolt/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L747) · [Engine ownership](../../../GEngine/include/GEngine/Physics/PassiveRagdoll.h#L56)

Leave optional `Stabilize()` off. On this 6.5 kg star its mass-ratio clamp would change the 2 kg torso to 1.30 kg and each child to 1.04 kg, then adjust inertia. `CalculateConstraintPriorities` gives all five leaf joints the same base priority. Keep stable insertion order. Use the existing layer/mask and editable `collideConnected` filter. Jolt's parent-child table can suppress the five adjacent pairs, but its pose-based overload can also suppress nearby nonadjacent parts. [Stabilization, priorities and filters](../../../external/jolt/Jolt/Physics/Ragdoll/Ragdoll.cpp#L51) · [Engine filter](../../../GEngine/src/Physics/JoltBackend.cpp#L892)

The tick-33 leg advanced 91.667 mm from a 33.321 mm gap. `Discrete` relies on ordinary speculative contact. The reference already used `LinearCast`, with 20 mm speculation, a 187.5 mm cast trigger and 20 mm permitted depth. Swapping joints leaves this miss exposed. For the **dedicated Gameplay world only**, propose `mLinearCastThreshold = 0.08` and `mLinearCastMaxPenetration = 0.02` through public `PhysicsSystem::SetPhysicsSettings`. At the 250 mm reference inner radius these give a 20 mm trigger and a 5 mm allowance at an ideal straight-on hit. Recheck effective values for edited radii. Archived awake velocities exceed the proposed trigger on 699 body samples across 170 ticks, up to six in one tick, versus zero at the original trigger. Actual casts and cost are unmeasured. At tick 172 a different corner of an already-contacting leg rotated into the floor. Stock casts hold orientation fixed and can cancel stale hits. Neither completed depth nor post-impulse joint recovery follows from the proposed settings.
