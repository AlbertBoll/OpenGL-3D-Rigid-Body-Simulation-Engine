## Bounded joint update

1. Only a successfully bound, generation-checked Jolt ragdoll may select the coupled path for its two limited-CV shoulders. After all six bodies and five joints pass existing construction and provider readback, atomically register their world-qualified handles. Copy handles into the owning world. Remove registration before member retirement, failed replacement, Reset and world close as applicable. A failed new-scene transaction preserves the old live registration. Never recognize this path by name, material, shape or apparent joint count.
2. In the existing Jolt constraint dispatch, form three anchor-velocity rows and the book quaternion equality row. For the U and V limits, retain the current principal frame, finite-step activation, impulse signs and permitted interior motion. Build the local effective-mass matrix `K = J M⁻¹ Jᵀ` from both bodies at their real COMs. Factor the four bilateral rows once per linearization. Eliminate them to obtain a system of at most two unilateral rows. Enumerate at most nine combinations of lower, inactive or upper limits. Accept a finite, rank-valid solution satisfying the signed total-impulse and complementary-velocity conditions. Scale rows for conditioning. Resolve ties deterministically.
3. Warm-start once with the saved **total** multipliers. Apply only their change to both bodies, with equal and opposite point impulses and the corresponding COM torques. Preserve effective joint readback, side transitions, reset and save/restore semantics. Keep position multipliers separate from physical impulse readback. Recompute frames/Jacobians after finite rotations in each existing position pass. A numerical failure must propagate as a typed step failure before publishing invalid state. Do not silently recover by projecting the final render pose.
4. Keep one 1/60 s Update, ten velocity and two position passes. At most two shoulders × twelve passes × nine small active-set candidates gives **216 candidate evaluations per reference Update**. One possible fixed binary64 workspace uses 2,048 bytes of scalar arrays (two 6×12 arrays, a 6×6 matrix, 4×4 factor, 4×2 and 2×2 reductions, eight 6-vectors). This excludes alignment, temporaries and stored multipliers. Verify actual layout/stack and construction ownership before admission. Allocate nothing per iteration. Preserve the five constraint pointers and existing SDK temporary-site and ABI bounds. CPU and visual stiffness require measurement on a candidate. [Existing Book-CV rows, warm start and readback](../../../../GEngine/src/Physics/JoltBackend.cpp#L526) · [Generation-safe Bind/Retire](../../../../GEngine/src/Physics/PassiveRagdoll.cpp#L110)

The SDK constraint solve returns a Boolean indicating whether it applied an impulse. A future candidate must carry invalid-rank/nonfinite faults separately. Validate the complete local impulse before applying any part, latch a fixed per-constraint fault until the owning adapter finishes Update, and surface it through the existing typed world step result. Prove that a failed step cannot be presented as valid or continued from partially advanced state. Stop at a source-ownership decision if the existing boundary cannot enforce that. ## Contact admission and rotational feasibility

Stock LinearCast is a fixed-orientation translation **after** rotation. Its allowed penetration is `min(slop, castMaxPenetration × innerRadius)`. As a sensitivity calculation only, a 10 mm translation trigger and allowance correspond to factors of 0.04 for the frozen 250 mm radius. In the archived motion, 974 saved body velocities across 204 ticks exceed 10 mm per step, compared with none above 187.5 mm. Six bodies can cross the lower trigger in one tick. These are counterfactual candidate counts on saved velocities, not actual casts or CPU timings. Velocities immediately before sleep are unavailable on 12 body updates. Even zero allowed translational penetration cannot prevent the tick-172 rotation in the recorded pose. A proposed future world-local profile needs separate input-policy approval if it changes a frozen setting. [SDK integration](../../../../external/jolt/Jolt/Physics/PhysicsSystem.cpp#L1641) · [Held-motion coverage](../../../../logs/pre_editor/phase-29/ragdoll-resume-02/floor-motion-review.md)

Screen a proposed integration seam for three things **before implementation or an RBS reservation**: admission of a pair outside the initial speculative range, admission of a new rotating support feature on a cached pair, and response to the post-impulse trajectory before the offending integration. Test the reasoning against positive-gap impulses, finite floor edges, walls, filtered neighbors, nonadjacent bones and supported authored shapes. The public shape cast returns hits. The pair callback follows broadphase and may be bypassed by cache reuse. Cache invalidation can request a new pair test, but cannot move it after a later impulse or submit the missing rotating feature by itself. The diagnostics listener observes contacts after detection. None of these seams establishes all three properties. A changed first impact may change later motion, but the retained capture cannot predict it. [Public cast query](../../../../external/jolt/Jolt/Physics/Collision/NarrowPhaseQuery.h#L67) · [Pair admission and cache](../../../../external/jolt/Jolt/Physics/PhysicsSystem.cpp#L967) · [Cache invalidation](../../../../external/jolt/Jolt/Physics/Body/BodyInterface.h#L313)

If a viable public seam cannot be shown, keep the replacement unused. A separate SDK decision would need a minimal insertion point before contact response, a finite bound on repeated contacts and changed trajectories, cache/manifold ownership, single restitution, approved shape coverage, fixed storage, compatibility with the existing pinned extension and a rollback path. The 40 reference candidate pairs bound pair identities only. Two substeps already require at least 23 positive reservations against 19. SDK work, increased substeps, warm-start disabling and higher iteration counts are outside this design request.
