## Proposed decision rule

1. Score every center under V01. For each joint, group consecutive above-limit centers `a…b`. Use its own frozen length `L` and `h = 0.001 × L`. Take the world-anchor distances `g` for samples `a−1…b+1`. Report the signed `g[t] − (g[t−1]+g[t+1])/2`, the raw peak gap, recovery and any simultaneous absolute-cap violation. The source columns are `ax…az` and `bx…bz`, in metres. Collapse exactly equal adjacent gaps only when counting interior local maxima. Record a repeated opening when two successive maxima have an intervening fall and subsequent rise both exceeding `h`. Keep endpoints and nearby recurrences visible in the full trace. [Raw joint samples](../../../logs/pre_editor/phase-29/resume-01/qualification/attempt-03-R-Jolt-R29_DROP_WAKE_V01/data/joints.csv)
2. Link the classification to the original signed rows and independent body-pose reconstruction. Missing samples, a disagreement on threshold crossing, or unavailable visual provenance leave the interpretation unresolved. One peak can still produce a visible pop. The quiet-window jitter, all-tick contact and joint caps, angular relation and limit checks, energy, sleep, integrity and cost verdicts retain their existing force.

A later scoring change would need preregistered physical amplitude and recovery bounds, two fixed camera projections at normal speed, identity-masked ratings of natural impacts and independently identified stretch, pop and repeated jitter, and successful held-out validation. Use existing matched footage where available. Reject a rule that clears an agreed visible defect. A separately approved, versioned scoring decision would be required before applying it to a candidate. [Calibration and held-out requirements](PHASE_29_POST_NATIVE_ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW_01.md)
