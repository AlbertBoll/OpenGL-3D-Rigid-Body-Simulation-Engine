## Jolt engineering boundary for both options

Start with the pinned Jolt 5.6.0 public integration. Retain one Update at 1/60 s, five joints, 10 velocity passes and two position passes. For the recorded 250 mm inner radius, a trigger strictly below the observed travel/radius ratio (about 0.36667) admits the first translation. A permitted-depth factor at most 0.04 bounds the cast allowance to 10 mm. These inequalities describe one flat impact. They do not establish completed penetration or authorize changing the frozen profile. Stock Jolt rotates before casting translation. Its pair callback runs downstream of broadphase and can be bypassed by a cached contact. Two internal collision steps already need at least 23 positive Arena reservations against the admitted 19. Preserve local frames, free swing, impulse signs, readback and a single warm start. Check conditioning at both limits. A connected arm must still swing and release naturally. Require a finite work bound and a typed failure on exhaustion. No complete upstream-preserving fix or compliant extension has been established. ## Proposed Game-Oriented Visual Acceptance Amendment

1. **Hard gates.** Preserve physical inputs, finite normalized poses, identity, lifetime/rollback, render/provider parity, actual contact response, energy/work, sleep/wake, joint-limit coverage and cost. Keep 10 mm transient and 5 mm quiet penetration. Overlap above 5 mm must recover by four ticks and remain recovered for the following two. Keep per-joint and summed anchor limits, 0.010/0.005 rad transient/quiet angular limits and all jitter limits. Compare each backend to its own workload. [Frozen criteria](../../../logs/pre_editor/phase-29/admission-02/criteria.json)
2. **Visibility and persistence.** For each excursion record pair/joint ID, onset/peak/recovery ticks, consecutive duration, recurrences, depth/limb thickness, anchor gap and signed three-sample residual. Retain the quiet-window sliding-60 peak-to-peak and jitter measures. Project the affected silhouette and endpoints into two preregistered views. Bind viewport extent, camera pose/projection, display scale, interpolation and occlusion. A brief or subpixel excursion remains subject to every hard gate.
3. **Owner verdict.** At normal speed, overlays off, inspect falling, tumbling, support and settling. A repeatable detached pivot, penetration, alternating snap, early rebound, skating, locked swing or unstable rest blocks visual acceptance. For a disputed interval, record view, action, ticks and bone. Use the second view and overlays to check closing/rebound normal speed, tangential slip and rolling contact-point velocity (`v + ω × r`). Normal bounce and slide are permissible. Record each applicable observation as PASS, FAIL or PENDING on the exact executable. [Standing visual-safety policy](../active/PHASE_26_OD08_GAME_QUALITY_AMENDMENT_01.json) · [Owner inspection method](../active/PHASE_29_OWNER_UI_METHOD_REVIEW_01.md)
4. **Short-lived residuals.** A transient within all limits that recovers on time and produces no repeatable visual defect can pass. To propose replacing a metric such as loaded anchor jitter, first demonstrate that it misclassifies natural motion. Preregister cameras, speed and the proposed physical bound. Have the Owner rate identity-masked clips with independent examples of natural swing, sustained stretch and alternating jitter. Test on held-out clips and reject a rule that would clear agreed visible defects. No perceptual tolerance is established by the current captures.
5. **Inspection coverage.** E-J and E-N each allow six seconds of simulated time (360 cumulative ticks). Inspect early impacts, Gizmos, joint/material editing, Apply/Revert/Reset, impulses and switching there. Use the 1,200-tick captures for late settling, tick-961 wake and resleep. Label offline playback as reconstruction. Direct Editor observation beyond 360 ticks requires an amendment. Retain all 976 row IDs, 488 per backend, with genuine applicable readbacks. Under B, E-N still covers Native authoring, experimental disclosure, impulses and controls. The proposed prerequisite for these sessions is Jolt A–C plus retained Native lifetime, integrity and safe-UI prerequisites. Any unresolved shared safety blocker holds both sessions.
