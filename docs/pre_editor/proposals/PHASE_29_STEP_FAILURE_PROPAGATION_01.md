## Defect and compatibility constraint

The adapter already returns a typed error. After synchronous SDK Update it checks callback identity and Jolt's error mask, calls the success diagnostic's `Finish`, writes a body's pose, velocities and sleep state, then tests `HasFiniteState`. A failure on the final body leaves earlier mirrors and their synchronization caches published and the invalid body partly written. PhysicsSystem keeps the error and withholds its completed count, but its public Update returns void. Scene rotates history before Update, then consumes backlog and increments its clocks unconditionally. The RBS fixture advances its own tick before checking completion and dispatches its success observers before the ordinary error handler. This is a source-supported fault path. All 1,200 historical Jolt DROP ticks completed with finite states.
